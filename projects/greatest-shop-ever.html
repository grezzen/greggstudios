Apparel & Hat Design

Created original graphics and designs for The Greatest Shop Ever's apparel and headwear collections. Developed artwork for garments and hats with a focus on creating distinctive designs that aligned with the shop's visual identity and retail audience.

Skateboard Initiative

Developed the visual designs for a skateboard initiative featuring custom board graphics produced for The Greatest Shop Ever. Created artwork specifically for physical skateboard production, adapting graphic concepts to the unique dimensions and shape of the boards while maintaining a cohesive visual identity across the collection.

Glass Blowing Events

Retail & Promotional Materials

Designed a broad range of promotional materials supporting The Greatest Shop Ever's retail marketing efforts, including direct-mail pieces, business cards, flyers, VIP cards, and other customer-facing collateral. Developed materials across multiple formats while maintaining consistency with the shop's overall brand and visual style.
