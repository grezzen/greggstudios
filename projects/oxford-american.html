Concert Series
Developed a complete promotional campaign for the Oxford American concert series, creating coordinated print and digital materials including mailers, web assets, flyers, and posters. Created a consistent look across the series while giving each event and performer its own visual presence.
50 Years of True Grit
Designed promotional and event materials for the Oxford American's three-day celebration of 50 Years of True Grit. Created a coordinated campaign spanning mailers, website assets, posters, flyers, and the event program. Developed the visual system to support multiple events while presenting the film's history and programming in a cohesive, professional format.
Giving Tuesday
Developed promotional materials for three Oxford American Giving Tuesday fundraising campaigns, each built around a distinct message and audience. 25 for 25 brought together 25 musicians with the goal of raising $25,000; the Women Writers Fund focused on supporting and highlighting women writers; and We Can Because She Can celebrated the women of the past whose work helped create opportunities for women today. Created a unique visual approach for each campaign while maintaining the Oxford American’s established identity across digital outreach and donor communications.
Promotional Pieces
Created promotional materials supporting the Oxford American's events, programs, and audience outreach. Developed coordinated print and digital pieces that combined editorial imagery, typography, and campaign messaging into clear, engaging communications while maintaining consistency with the publication's broader visual identity.
