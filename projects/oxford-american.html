Oxford American

Design work created for the Oxford American across multiple events and promotional campaigns. Projects included concert series materials, event programs, mailers, posters, flyers, and digital assets developed to support programming and audience outreach.

Concert Series

Promotional materials developed for the Oxford American Concert Series, including mailers, web assets, flyers, and posters. The campaign required a consistent visual system that could accommodate multiple performers and events while maintaining a recognizable identity across print and digital applications.

50 Years of True Grit

Design and promotional materials for the Oxford American's three-day celebration of 50 Years of True Grit. Created a coordinated campaign spanning mailers, web assets, posters, flyers, and the event program. Developed the visual system to support multiple events while presenting the full history and programming in a cohesive, professional format.
